# MoonKeep (טירת הירח) - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

This policy explains how the MoonKeep iOS game handles your information.

## Information we collect

MoonKeep does not ask for your name, email, phone number or location, and has no user accounts. Your game progress (levels, score, coins, characters) is saved only on your device.

## In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see or store your payment details. We use RevenueCat to validate purchases and restore them. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, your purchase history and basic device information (such as iOS version and country). See the RevenueCat privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

## Advertising and tracking

MoonKeep shows no ads, does not track you across other apps or websites, and does not sell or share your data.

## Children

MoonKeep does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page.

## Contact

For any question about this policy, contact us through the support link on the MoonKeep App Store page.
